## 📑 CORE CHAPTER STRUCTURE (DOMAIN-AGNOSTIC)

### Section 1: First Principles & Theoretical Foundations
- **Foundational Definition:** Define `[TOPIC_TITLE]` from first principles. Why does this field/topic exist, and what fundamental problem does it solve?
- **Historical & Theoretical Context:** Trace the evolution of thought, key intellectual breakthroughs, and foundational axioms.
- **The Conceptual Gap:** Contrast naive/popular misunderstandings with advanced, rigorous domain reality.
- **Core Axioms & Governing Laws:** Detail the essential rules, governing principles, or foundational formulas that define this domain.

### Section 2: Core Mechanics & Structural Breakdown
- **Systematic Deconstruction:** Break the topic down into its 3–5 fundamental sub-components or pillars.
- **Internal Dynamics & Interactions:** Detail how these components interact dynamically under normal conditions versus extreme conditions.
- **Visual / Conceptual Diagrams:** Provide Mermaid diagrams, workflow trees, or structural maps illustrating component relationships.
- **Taxonomy & Terminology:** Provide a precise, standardized glossary table of essential domain terminology.

### Section 3: Diagnostic Frameworks & Analytical Tradeoff Analysis
- **Analytical Frameworks:** Introduce 2–3 standard analytical frameworks or diagnostic lenses used by experts in this field.
- **Comparative Evaluation Matrix:** Construct a detailed matrix comparing 3–4 competing paradigms, theories, tools, or methodologies across key dimensions (e.g., efficiency, complexity, scope, risk).
- **Edge Cases & Failure Modes:** Analyze what happens when the underlying assumptions break down or fail under stress.

### Section 4: Mathematical, Formal, or Empirical Models
- **Formal Notation & Equations:** State and derive key mathematical formulas, statistical distributions, or logical proofs governing the topic.
- **Worked Numerical / Theoretical Examples:** Walk step-by-step through a concrete, fully solved problem or scenario.
- **Statistical / Analytical Fallacies:** Highlight common analytical errors made when interpreting data or applying theory in this field.

### Section 5: Real-World Applications & Case Studies
- **In-Depth Case Study 1:** Analyze a famous success story or historic event demonstrating perfect application of these principles.
- **In-Depth Case Study 2:** Analyze a famous failure mode, crisis, or anti-pattern caused by violating these principles.
- **Best Practices & Golden Rules:** Summarize actionable heuristics, rules of thumb, and design patterns.

### Section 6: Classroom Workshop, Case-Study Script & Instructor Manual
- **Minute-by-Minute Teaching Timeline:** Provide a 60-minute interactive lesson plan (0–15m Introduction & Foundations, 15–30m Deep Dive, 30–45m Live Exercise, 45–60m Synthesis & Q&A).
- **Interactive Workshop Exercise / Scenario Cards:** Create 2–3 hands-on incident/case-study cards detailing:
  - *Scenario & Context*
  - *Symptom / Dilemma Presented*
  - *Analytical Query / Investigation Steps*
  - *Optimal Solution & Remediation*
